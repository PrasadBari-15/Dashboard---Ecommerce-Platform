# E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance and generate meaningful business insights from sales data.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key sales and profitability metrics, helping users understand business performance across different dimensions such as states, customers, payment modes, categories, and subcategories.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Track sales quantity and monthly profit trends.
- Identify high-performing states and customers.
- Analyze sales based on different payment modes.
- Compare product categories and subcategories.
- Present key business metrics through an interactive dashboard.

## 📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard includes important performance indicators such as:

- **Sales Amount**
- **Profit**
- **Quantity Sold**
- **Monthly Profit**
- **Customer Performance**
- **Category & Subcategory Performance**

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### Sales Analysis
Analyze overall sales performance and identify trends across different periods.

### Profit Analysis
Track profit generation and understand monthly changes in profitability.

### State-wise Analysis
Compare sales performance across different states to identify high-performing regions.

### Customer Analysis
Analyze customer-level sales performance and identify major contributors to revenue.

### Payment Mode Analysis
Understand sales distribution across different payment methods.

### Category & Subcategory Analysis
Compare product categories and subcategories based on sales and profitability.
